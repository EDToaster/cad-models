# Small MUJI spray bottle

Owner's measurements: **24.40 mm diameter × 86 mm tall**. For the layout, treat these as the outside diameter and overall parked height. The reference is a plain upright cylinder centered on the Z axis, with its base at Z=0. The cap and sprayer were not measured separately.

Use the cylinder at nominal size. In the full tray mockup, try a shallow round recess in the deck rather than a separate cup; choose its depth and print clearance after checking stability and lift-out access. The 24.40 mm diameter is not the printed pocket size.

Build from `src/` with `cad run -m equipment.coffee.muji_spray_bottle`.
