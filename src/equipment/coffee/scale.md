# Coffee scale reference

The caliper envelope in weighing position is **128.2 mm wide × 148.4 mm long × 23.5 mm thick**, including the silicone feet. In storage the scale stands on a 148.4 mm edge, making it 128.2 mm tall. Positive Z is up in both configurations.

The whole body is the weighing platform. The four separate silicone feet transmit force to the load cell, so the model has one rigid weighing body and four external feet, with no separate top plate or internal sensor geometry. The underside photograph sets the approximate corner radius, foot diameter and insets. Foot projection is a provisional 2 mm for visualization only: the full 23.5 mm includes it. Use the overall envelope, not the approximate foot geometry, for the storage fit.

From `src/`, run `cad run -m equipment.coffee.scale` to build both poses. The reusable equipment model contains no tray allowance. Store the scale on its long body edge, without gripping the feet or squeezing the faces. The tray is for storage, not weighing.
