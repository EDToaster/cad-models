# Espresso dosing ring

The owner described a simple ring: **62 mm outside diameter, 53 mm inside diameter and 28 mm height**. The resulting radial wall is 4.5 mm thick. Model a straight, concentric through bore with no taper or stepped lip.

Build from `src/` with `cad run -m equipment.coffee.dosing_ring`. The ring axis is +Z, centered on the origin, with the lower rim on Z=0. Dimensions are nominal equipment geometry; holder clearances and the final storage orientation belong to the tray assembly.
