# Normcore tamper cap

The tray holds the **cap**, not the bare tamper. Its measured outer envelope is **64 mm diameter × 13 mm high**. The nominal 58.5 mm identifies the owner's [Normcore Spring Loaded Tamper V4 with Ripple Base](https://www.normcorewares.com/products/normcore-spring-loaded-tamper-v4-with-ripple-base); it is not the tray interface or a measured inside diameter.

The reusable model is a solid cylindrical envelope centered on the Z axis with its underside at Z=0. It does not represent the cap's internal recess, wall thickness, or any geometry of the tamper sitting above it. Use it to place the cap in the full tray mockup, not to manufacture a replica or check the complete tamper's overhead clearance.

Build from `src/` with `cad run -m equipment.coffee.normcore_tamper_cap`. Holder clearance and pocket depth belong to the tray and are deferred until the full mockup.
